Currency Value Over Time
Currency shifts value over time due to various factors including economic policies, technological advancements, and geopolitical events. This timeline illustrates significant moments that have influenced the value of money throughout history. When the value increased we color it green and when it decreased it is red. Significant points are labeled as dots on the timeline.
Asset Performance Bubbles
Explore how different assets (Bitcoin, S&P 500, Gold, Oil, and USD Index) performed over time. The size of each bubble represents the daily percentage change. Use the timeline controls to see how assets responded to major historical events.
Interactive Timeline
Click the play button or drag the slider to navigate through time. Toggle assets on/off using the checkboxes on the right. Hover over bubbles to see detailed information about price and daily changes.
Meet the Assets
Each financial asset has its own personality—shaped by volatility, performance, and behavior during crises. Scroll to discover what makes each one unique.
Bitcoin: A Rocket with No Seatbelt
Bitcoin is explosive, unpredictable, and reactive—capable of extreme booms and catastrophic crashes within days. Its extreme volatility makes it both thrilling and terrifying.
Gold: The Steady Grandma
Gold is calm. When panic spreads, it stabilizes portfolios and moves gently upward. It's the asset you turn to when everything else is falling.
Oil: The Mood-Swinging Uncle
Oil reacts to war, supply cuts, and global demand. It can skyrocket or collapse depending on geopolitics. Never boring, always unpredictable.
S&P 500: The Dependable Workhorse
S&P 500 reflects human economic activity. It grows in good times, falls in global uncertainty. The heartbeat of corporate America.
USD Index: The Global Referee
The U.S. dollar is the world's safe haven. When everything breaks, people run to the dollar. Always stable, always trusted.
